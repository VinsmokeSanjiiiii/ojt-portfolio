Reflection & Learning Insights

What I learned during OJT

During my internship, I made significant progress in my interpersonal communication, transitioning from a reserved nature to confidently engaging with diverse individuals and addressing inquiries professionally. My technical foundation in Information Technology was broadened through hands-on applications, moving from theoretical concepts to practical implementations.

I gained proficiency in the installation and maintenance of CCTV systems, ensuring consistent security coverage for the organization. Furthermore, I managed network configurations, including router setup and optimization, to maintain connectivity across departments. I also took responsibility for inventory encoding, which improved my attention to detail and digital asset management.


Overall Reflection

This internship served as a bridge between my academic studies in Information Technology and the demands of the professional industry. I developed greater self-confidence, transitioning from a student mindset to that of a proactive professional capable of handling real-world responsibilities.

Collaborating within the MIS department taught me the value of teamwork and the importance of seeking mentorship to achieve career goals. I also gained a disciplined work ethic, particularly regarding the documentation and reporting required in IT systems maintenance.
